What Makes Scenic 30A Special?

Friends · Sep 12

A Collection of Distinct Communities

Scenic 30A passes through communities with their own history, architecture and character. Preserving the corridor includes respecting that individuality.

An Extraordinary Natural Environment

State parks, Point Washington State Forest, wetlands, beaches and coastal dune lakes place developed communities remarkably close to protected natural areas.

A Corridor Meant to Be Experienced

The Timpoochee Trail, state parks and forest trails make walking, bicycling and exploring part of the 30A experience.

More Than a Scenic Road

The corridor's scenic, natural, historic, cultural and recreational resources are the reason continued thoughtful planning and community involvement matter.
