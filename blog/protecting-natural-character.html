Native Vegetation Matters

Native vegetation helps define the visual character of Scenic 30A while supporting wildlife habitat and an ecosystem adapted to local soil, weather and coastal conditions. Friends has historically encouraged native vegetation preservation, responsible landscaping and public education.

Protecting Heritage Trees and Scenic Character

The scenic character of a roadway can gradually change through many small decisions. Friends has supported heritage-tree protection, thoughtful landscaping and screening of infrastructure where appropriate.

Our Coastal Dune Lakes

Coastal dune lakes are a defining feature of South Walton. Protecting them requires stewardship extending beyond the water's edge because development, landscaping, drainage, public use and infrastructure around a lake can affect the resource.

State Lands and Trails

State parks, Point Washington State Forest and trails preserve natural landscapes while giving residents and visitors ways to experience them.

Preservation Requires Participation

Property owners, businesses, visitors, community organizations and government all play a role in protecting what makes Scenic 30A distinctive.
