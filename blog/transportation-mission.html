A Longstanding Role in Transportation Planning

Transportation planning and safety have long been among Friends' areas of work, including participation in regional planning and coordination on corridor recommendations.

Making 30A Safer for People Walking and Biking

Friends has advocated for trail improvements, safer crossings, bicycle facilities, wayfinding and better trail visibility.

Connectivity Matters

Scenic 30A cannot be considered in isolation. Connections to US 98, communities, businesses, beaches and emergency routes all affect the corridor.

Growth Makes the Conversation More Important

Transportation solutions should improve safety, connectivity and mobility while respecting the natural resources, communities and scenic character that make 30A different.

Planning for the Future

Long-term solutions may involve pedestrian and bicycle infrastructure, safer intersections, improved trail connections, parking strategies, roadway maintenance and regional connectivity.
