OUR IMPACT

Protecting and Enhancing Scenic 30A

For more than two decades, Friends of Scenic 30A has worked to preserve the character, natural beauty, history, and quality of life that make the Scenic 30A corridor special.

Major Accomplishments

Florida Scenic Highway Designation

Friends played a leading role in the effort that resulted in Scenic 30A receiving official Florida Scenic Highway designation in 2008.

National Scenic Byway Designation

Friends helped prepare and advance the successful application that resulted in National Scenic Byway designation in 2021.

Friends’ Corner at Eastern Lake

Friends spearheaded a landscaped rest area for pedestrians and bicyclists at Scenic 30A and Eastern Lake Road.

Transportation, Trails & Safety

Timpoochee Trail & Safety

Supporting safer crossings, trail improvements, bicycle facilities, better signage and expanded multimodal connections.

Transportation & Connectivity

Participating in discussions about roadway safety, connections, traffic, emergency access and regional mobility while supporting 30A's two-lane scenic character.

Wayfinding & Signage

Working toward cohesive wayfinding, safety signage and reduced visual clutter.

Beautification & Preservation

30A Gateway Improvements

Landscaping and gateway improvements celebrating state and national scenic designations.

Natural Resources

Advocating for coastal dune lakes, native vegetation, state parks and Point Washington State Forest.

Scenic & Historic Character

Preserving heritage trees, historic resources, scenic views and thoughtful infrastructure.
