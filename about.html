ABOUT FRIENDS OF SCENIC 30A

Preserving What Makes 30A Special

We are a community-led organization dedicated to maintaining the unique character, natural beauty, and environmental health of the Scenic Highway 30A corridor for all who live here and visit.

Our Story

Friends of Scenic 30A serves as the designated Byway Organization for Scenic Highway 30A. Since Scenic 30A received its Florida Scenic Highway designation in 2008, Friends has helped carry forward a community vision centered on preserving the corridor's extraordinary natural, scenic, historic, recreational and cultural resources.

Friends works with residents, businesses, Walton County, the Tourist Development Council and other community and regional partners on transportation and trail safety, signage and wayfinding, beautification, public education, community engagement and thoughtful improvements throughout the Scenic 30A corridor.

In 2021, Scenic 30A received National Scenic Byway designation. Today, Friends continues working to protect what makes Scenic 30A special while helping prepare the corridor for the challenges and opportunities ahead.

Our Mission

Protect. Preserve. Enhance.

Our mission is to help preserve and enhance the natural, scenic, recreational, historic, and community resources that define Scenic Highway 30A.

Protecting Natural Resources

Supporting preservation of coastal dune lakes, native vegetation, forests, dunes, beaches and natural landscapes.

Preserving Scenic 30A

Supporting thoughtful planning, landscaping, infrastructure and maintenance that respect the corridor.

Trails, Walking & Biking

Supporting trail improvements, pedestrian connections, crossings, signage and safer transportation infrastructure.

Community & Heritage

Celebrating and preserving the places, stories and character that make the corridor unique.

Education & Stewardship

Engaging residents, visitors, businesses and community leaders in protecting Scenic 30A's resources.

Preserving 30A for Generations to Come

Our goal is not to prevent change, but to help ensure that change occurs thoughtfully. What we protect today becomes the 30A of tomorrow.
